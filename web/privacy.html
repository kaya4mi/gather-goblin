Privacy

Room names, member names, preferences, notes, routes entered by members and votes are stored to provide shared planning. People with a room link can read the shared room. The person selected for conditional attendance is private to that member. Device-held edit tokens are not shown to other visitors.

Your device stores a membership token, language choice, profile preferences and recent room links. Clearing browser storage creates a new device identity.

When in-app lunch search is enabled and you submit a search, your entered location and food preferences are sent to Google Maps Platform to find places and calculate walking routes. Google place results are displayed for that search and are not saved in our room database. Aggregate daily request counts are stored to control service usage. Opening a Google Maps link sends the link’s locations and search terms to Google.

Google services are governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Share private addresses and personal notes only with people you intend to invite.
